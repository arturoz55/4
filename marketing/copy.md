## Tweets

**1. How the fee works (attach `hyperpad-how.png`)**
> Every trade on a Hyperpad coin pays 1%.
>
> 0.3% goes to the launcher, in ETH.
> 0.5% fills the coin's reel jar.
> 0.2% goes to Hyperpad.
>
> When the jar hits $25, a new AI reel starts rendering. The more a coin trades, the more it gets talked about.

**2. The reel (attach `hyperpad-reel.png`)**
> This reel cost $25 in trading fees.
>
> Nobody wrote a script or typed a prompt. The coin's name, ticker and one pitch line went into a format, and an AI host did the rest in about eight minutes.
>
> Ten reels later it looks like ten different people found your coin.

**3. ZEC tips (attach `hyperpad-zec.png`)**
> Fans can now tip any Hyperpad coin in ZEC.
>
> Shielded tips hide the sender, the receiver and the amount, and they can carry an encrypted memo, like "make the next one an unboxing".
>
> Every tip goes straight into the reel jar.

**4. The formats**
> Eight reel formats, picked in rotation so a coin never gets the same one twice in a row:
>
> talking head
> unboxing
> street interview
> hot take
> reaction
> explainer
> skit
> podcast clip
>
> Which one should $HPAD get next?

**5. The feed**
> The Hyperpad feed has one rule: the newest reel plays first, full screen, with a buy button under it.
>
> Coins that keep trading keep getting reels.
> Coins that stop, drop out of view.
>
> Volume is the algorithm.

**6. For launchers**
> What you need to launch on Hyperpad:
>
> a name
> a ticker
> one line of pitch
> one image
>
> That's it. The reels, the hosts and the feed placement come from trading.
