Trade it. Film it. Feed it.

Launch a coin. Reels pitch it.

You set up the coin. Its trading fees pay AI hosts to make short videos about it. Every $25 in fees buys one more reel, with a new face and a new voice, posted straight to the feed.

$

You keep 0.3% of every trade, sent to your wallet in ETH. Another 0.5% pays for reels and 0.2% goes to Hypereel.

or
Browse the feed

  1. Set up in a minute

    Name, ticker, one pitch line and a picture. The coin goes live with its own card in the feed.

  2. Trades pay the hosts

    Half of every 1% fee goes to reels. Each $25 books a new AI host and a new format. Rendering takes about eight minutes.

  3. Busy coins stay on screen

    The feed plays the newest reel first, with a buy button under it. Coins that keep trading keep getting reels.

Formats

Eight reel formats, picked in rotation

A coin never gets the same format twice in a row, so ten reels look like ten different people found it.

Reels are made from fixed formats using only the coin's name, ticker, pitch line and image. No free-text prompts, no real people and no third-party brands. The hosts are AI. Launchers own their reels and can post them anywhere.
