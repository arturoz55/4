How it works

From launch to feed in three steps

  1. Step 1

    Set up in a minute

    Name, ticker, one pitch line and a picture. The coin goes live with its own card in the feed.

  2. Step 2

    Trades pay the hosts

    Half of every 1% fee goes to reels. Each $25 books a new AI host and a new format. Rendering takes about eight minutes.

  3. Step 3

    Busy coins stay on screen

    The feed plays the newest reel first, with a buy button under it. Coins that keep trading keep getting reels.

Formats

Eight reel formats, picked in rotation

A coin never gets the same format twice in a row, so ten reels look like ten different people found it.

Reels are made from fixed formats using only the coin's name, ticker, pitch line and image. No free-text prompts, no real people and no third-party brands. The hosts are AI. Launchers own their reels and can post them anywhere.

Zcash tips

Tip a coin in ZEC, and keep it private

Any coin can take tips in Zcash. Every tip goes straight into the coin's reel jar, so fans can pay for the next reel without trading.

  • Shielded by default. A shielded tip hides the sender, the receiver and the amount on chain.
  • Encrypted memo. Add up to 512 bytes of text that only the coin's owner can read.
  • Any Zcash wallet. Scan the QR code. It is a standard ZIP-321 payment request with the amount and memo filled in.

      Questions

      What people ask before they launch

      What does it cost to launch a coin?

      Nothing up front. Hyperpad takes its share from trading fees: every trade pays 1%, split 0.3% to you, 0.5% to reels and 0.2% to Hyperpad.

      Who makes the reels?

      AI hosts. Each reel uses one of eight fixed formats and only your coin's name, ticker, pitch line and image. There are no free-text prompts and no real people in the videos.

      How often does my coin get a new reel?

      Every time $25 of reel fees builds up in its jar. At $10,000 of daily volume that is two reels a day. ZEC tips fill the same jar, so fans can speed it up.

      Why Zcash for tips?

      Shielded Zcash payments hide the sender, the receiver and the amount on chain, and they can carry an encrypted memo. Fans can back a coin without putting their wallet history on display.

      Can I post the reels somewhere else?

      Yes. Launchers own the reels made for their coin and can post them anywhere.

      Is this page live?

      No. This page is a demo with example coins and simulated trading. No tokens are created and no money or ZEC moves.
