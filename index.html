How it works

From launch to feed in three steps

  1. 1

    Launch in about a minute

    Pick a name, a ticker, one line of pitch and one image. Your coin goes live with its own card in the feed.

  2. 2

    Trading funds the reels

    Each trade pays a 1% fee into your coin's vault. When the reel share reaches $25, a new reel starts rendering. It takes about eight minutes.

  3. 3

    The feed follows the fees

    The newest reel plays first, full screen, with a buy button under it. Coins that keep trading keep getting reels. Coins that stop drop out of view.

Formats

Eight reel formats, picked in rotation

A coin never gets the same format twice in a row, so ten reels look like ten different people found it.

Reels are made from fixed formats using only the coin's name, ticker, pitch line and image. No free-text prompts, no real people and no third-party brands. The hosts are AI. Launchers own their reels and can post them anywhere.
